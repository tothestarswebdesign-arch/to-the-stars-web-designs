How a project runs

Five clear steps, a fixed timeline and no surprises. You always know what happens next.

  1. Brief

    Days 1–3

    A call to learn your business, audience and goals. You get a written plan and a fixed quote.

  2. Design

    Week 1

    We design your key pages in full. You review, comment and approve.

  3. Build

    Weeks 2–3

    We build it, add your content and test on real phones and browsers.

  4. Launch

    Week 3–4

    We connect your domain, go live and check everything works.

  5. Support

    Ongoing

    Training, fixes and optional monthly care so the site keeps performing.

What we need from you

  • Your logo and brand colors, if you have them
  • Photos of your work, team or products
  • A few notes on what you offer and who it's for
  • One person who can approve each step quickly

Good to know

What if I don't like the design?

You see and approve the design before we build. Two rounds of revisions are included.

How do payments work?

Half to start and half at launch. Bigger projects can be split into milestones.

Do you write the content?

We can help polish your text, or you can send it and we'll place it beautifully.

What happens after launch?

We train you to edit the site. You can also add a monthly care plan.
